# AHAD Softtech Corporate Website --- Website Requirements

## 1. Objective

Create a premium, conversion-oriented B2B corporate website that makes
AHAD Softtech immediately understandable to international prospects and
drives qualified BPO enquiries.

The website should feel:

-   Professional
-   Reliable
-   Global
-   Human
-   Operationally capable
-   Technology-enabled
-   Modern

It must not feel like a generic outsourced website template.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Homepage Requirements

### Hero

The hero must communicate:

1.  What AHAD does.
2.  Who it serves.
3.  Why a prospect should care.
4.  What action to take.

Recommended structure:

**Eyebrow:** Global BPO & Technology Solutions

**Headline:** A strong business-outcome statement focused on scalable
customer operations.

**Supporting copy:** Brief explanation of BPO, customer support,
technical support and technology capabilities.

**Primary CTA:** Request a Quote

**Secondary CTA:** Explore BPO Services

Optional supporting trust line:

> India-based delivery. Built for global businesses.

### Hero visual

Use premium human/operations imagery or an editorial composition
combining people and technology.

Avoid cliché stock imagery of headset agents staring at monitors.

## 12. WhatsApp

Persistent floating WhatsApp CTA.

Desktop:

-   Icon + optional label
-   Bottom/right placement
-   Avoid blocking important UI.

Mobile:

-   Floating button with safe-area spacing.

The prefilled message must be contextual.

Example:

> Hello AHAD Softtech, I am interested in your BPO services. I would
> like to discuss my requirements.

For a service page, replace the service dynamically.

## 13. Responsive Requirements

### Mobile

-   320px minimum practical support.
-   One-column forms.
-   Touch targets approximately 44px or greater.
-   Sticky/accessible primary CTA.
-   Simplified navigation.
-   Optimized imagery.

### Tablet

-   Two-column content where appropriate.
-   Adaptive grids.
-   Maintain generous whitespace.

### Desktop

-   Max-width content container.
-   Multi-column sections.
-   Hover interactions.
-   Mega-menu/dropdowns.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 14. Animation Requirements

Modern Premium animation system:

-   Hero entrance animation
-   Scroll-reveal sections
-   Number/count animation when real metrics exist
-   Card hover
-   Button micro-interactions
-   Image reveal
-   Navigation transitions
-   Accordion animation
-   Page transition where technically justified

Rules:

-   Keep animation short and purposeful.
-   Avoid excessive parallax.
-   Avoid motion on every element.
-   Support `prefers-reduced-motion`.
-   Never hide important content behind animation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 18. Website Acceptance Criteria

A page is complete when:

-   Content is understandable without prior knowledge of AHAD.
-   Primary CTA is visible.
-   Mobile layout is usable.
-   CMS content renders correctly.
-   Metadata is configurable.
-   Keyboard navigation works.
-   Images have appropriate alt text.
-   Loading/error states are handled.
-   Analytics events are emitted.
-   Performance is acceptable on mobile.
